# DLS Magician GTM success metrics

## Product promise
Turn product intent into structured, inspectable, reusable interaction logic that design and engineering can review together.

## North-star outcome
**Weekly active workspaces that create or materially edit a workflow and successfully inspect, share, or export its generated artifact.**

## Activation
An activated user:
1. signs in successfully;
2. opens or creates a workspace/project;
3. authors a valid GIVEN / WHEN / THEN flow; and
4. sees a valid generated artifact.

Target: **>= 60%** of new activated users reach a first valid workflow within **10 minutes**.

## Reliability
- valid structured-language -> generated-artifact success: >= 98%
- autosave + reload success: >= 99.5%
- crash/error-free core sessions: >= 99.8%
- zero known P0/P1 auth or data-loss defects
- production data must use durable, recoverable persistence before broad public launch

## Retention and collaboration
- W1 activated-workspace retention: >= 35%
- >= 30% of activated workspaces use comments, sharing, versions, or multi-user review in week 1
- >= 5 design-partner teams export/share an artifact into a downstream implementation workflow

## Commercial validation
Before broad paid acquisition:
- >= 10 real design-partner teams use DLS Magician on a real workflow;
- >= 5 teams use it more than once across separate work sessions;
- >= 40% of pilot teams say they would be disappointed if it disappeared;
- at least 3 teams identify a measurable reduction in handoff/rework or clarification time.

## Distribution
Track source, build, deployment, and store publication separately for:
- Web
- PWA
- Chrome
- iOS
- Android
- Production
- Store

A passing build is not a published distribution channel.
